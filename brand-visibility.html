Frequently Asked Questions

What is Answer Engine Optimization?

AEO is the practice of optimizing your brand's content and authority so that AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews select your brand as the answer when users ask questions in your category. It is the evolution of SEO for the AI answer era.

How long to appear in AI answers?

Entity and authority signals typically take 3–6 months to propagate across AI systems. We begin with a Visibility Audit to identify the fastest leverage points and prioritize accordingly. Early wins — like FAQ schema and structured content — can show up in weeks.

Is this different from traditional SEO?

Yes. Traditional SEO targets ranked blue links. Brand Visibility Expert targets the AI answer layer — the citations, knowledge panels, entity records, and authority signals that AI systems use to decide who to recommend. The two disciplines overlap but require distinct strategies.
