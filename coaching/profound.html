What Profound Does

Profound measures how your brand appears inside AI search — which prompts surface you, how often you're cited, who gets named instead, and how that moves over time across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews.

That's genuinely hard to do by hand at any scale. Running thirty prompts across four engines once is an afternoon. Running them weekly, tracking competitor share of voice, and spotting a drift in how you're described is not something a person does reliably.

What the tool cannot do is tell you why. It reports that you dropped out of a prompt's answer. It doesn't tell you whether that's because a competitor earned a placement in the source being cited, because your schema broke, or because the model changed how it weights recency. That interpretive step is where the value is, and it's the part teams skip.

What We Set Up

Prompt Design

The single highest-leverage decision. Track the prompts your buyers actually ask, weighted toward unbranded discovery. Most default prompt sets over-index on branded terms and produce a flattering, useless picture.

Reading The Data Honestly

High branded visibility is not good news on its own — it means the model can read your website. The gap between branded and unbranded performance is the actual finding.

Who gets named when you don't, and which sources keep producing them. This is where the placement target list comes from.

Which pages the engines cite for your category. Frequently competitor-authored listicles — meaning a competitor is effectively writing the answer about you.

notifications

Alerting Worth Having

Alerts on material change only. A tool that pings on every fluctuation gets muted in a fortnight, and then it may as well not exist.

A written interpretation, not a dashboard screenshot. Three things that changed, why, and what to do. Reporting formats covered in SEO coaching.

Common Questions

Do we need Profound, or can we do this manually?

You can baseline manually — a structured prompt set run by hand across four engines gives you a defensible starting point. What you can't do manually is track it weekly across hundreds of prompts. Tooling earns its place at monitoring, not at diagnosis.

We bought it and nobody uses it. Is that fixable?

It's the most common situation and yes. Unused tooling is almost never a tool problem — it's that no decision was ever attached to the dashboard. We start by defining what you'd actually do differently based on what it shows.

Can you run it for us instead?

Yes. Done-for-you covers setup, prompt design, weekly monitoring and a monthly read with recommendations. Most clients start there and move to coaching once someone internal can own it.

Are you certified?

Profound University certification is in progress. In the meantime the work stands on the audits — the methodology is published openly and you can read a full worked example before hiring me.
