What This Actually Is

An independent consultant, not an agency. When you hire me you work with me — the person doing the audit is the person on the call and the person writing the roadmap.

I am based in London, Ontario and work with businesses in Vadodara and across Gujarat remotely. That is stated plainly because local SEO runs on accurate information, and a consultant who fakes a local address is telling you something about how they will handle your data too.

What the remote arrangement actually costs you is a few hours of calendar overlap. What it buys is fifteen years of pattern recognition from campaigns in North American and Indian markets, applied to a market where most competitors are still optimising for ten blue links.

Where The Work Concentrates

01

AI Search Visibility

Your buyers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews rather than typing a query and scanning links. Those answers name two or three brands. Getting named is a different job from ranking, because the model assembles its answer largely from third-party sources rather than your own site.

02

Technical SEO

Crawl and index diagnostics, site architecture, internal linking, Core Web Vitals, and the structured data that lets search engines resolve what your pages are actually about. Audit the structure first, then build on solid ground.

03

eCommerce & SaaS Growth

Category and product architecture for stores competing against far larger budgets, and bottom-of-funnel content for software companies. Structure that compounds, rather than a campaign that expires when the retainer does.

04

Local Search In Gujarat

Google Business Profile, citation consistency across Indian directories, and location pages that earn their place instead of repeating one template with the city name swapped out.

How An Engagement Runs

A diagnostic call first. Thirty minutes, free, no sales script. You describe the business and what search is currently doing for it; I tell you what I would look at first and whether the problem is even SEO.

Then an audit. Technical state, content and keyword coverage, competitor positions, and — where it matters — a real prompt set run against the AI engines to see who currently gets named for the questions your buyers ask.

Then a roadmap you could hand to someone else. Sequenced by expected impact, with the reasoning attached, so it survives you changing your mind about who executes it.

Reporting carries baselines and timeframes. When something moves the wrong way, that gets reported too.
