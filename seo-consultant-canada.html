What This Actually Is

An independent SEO consultant, not an agency. You work with me directly — strategy, diagnosis and the difficult calls — which is a different product from a team of five juniors following a checklist someone else wrote.

Based in London, Ontario, working across Toronto, Canada, the United States and India. The Canadian market is home and the largest share of the work, but search doesn't respect borders and neither do most of the clients.

The work now spans two surfaces. Classic search — ranking, technical health, content that earns its position. And generative search — being named and cited when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews the same question they used to type into a search box. Those are related but not identical problems, and most consultants are still only solving the first one.

How An Engagement Runs

01
Diagnosis Before Prescription

A technical crawl, a Search Console review, and an honest read of the competitive position. Most engagements start by discovering the problem is not the one you were told it was.

02
Rank The Findings Ruthlessly

Every audit produces a hundred items and roughly four of them matter. You get the four, in order, with the reasoning — not a spreadsheet you'll never open again.

03
Fix The Foundation

Technical health and indexation first, because content published onto a broken foundation compounds the problem instead of the results.

04
Build Content That Earns Position

Briefs grounded in what actually ranks, not word-count targets pulled from a tool. The briefing method is published openly.

05
Cover The AI Surface

Entity definition, extractable answers, and the third-party placements that determine whether generative engines mention you at all.

06
Report In Business Terms

Revenue and pipeline, not ranking tables. This is what keeps search funded when budgets get reviewed.

Common Questions

Do you only work with Canadian companies?

No. Toronto is the base and the largest share of the work, but engagements run across North America and further out. Timezone overlap matters more than borders — most of the work is with teams between Eastern and Pacific.

Consultant or agency — what's the difference here?

You get me. There is no account manager relaying instructions to a junior, and no team you meet once during the pitch and never again. That constrains how many clients I take, which is the trade-off you're buying.

Do you handle AI search as well as Google?

Yes, and increasingly it's the more interesting half. Ranking first no longer guarantees being mentioned when someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity the same question — different measurement, different failure modes. Covered on the AI search visibility page.

What size company do you work with?

Anywhere from a funded startup with one marketer to an enterprise team with a search function that has stalled. The common factor is that someone senior needs to own the strategy and nobody currently does.

How quickly do results show?

Technical fixes can move things in weeks. Content and authority work runs on a quarter-to-quarter cycle. Anyone quoting a faster timeline on a competitive term is selling you something.
