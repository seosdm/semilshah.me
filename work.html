How Results Get Reported Here

01
The Starting Point, Stated

A jump from 200 to 1,000 monthly sessions is 400% growth and also almost nothing. Percentages without a baseline are the oldest trick in this industry and the easiest to spot.

02
The Timeframe, Stated

Fourteen months and fourteen weeks are different claims. Anyone omitting the period is hoping you'll assume the shorter one.

03
What Else Was Happening

A site that also launched a product, raised funding or tripled ad spend did not get that lift from SEO alone. Honest attribution costs credit and buys credibility.

04
What Didn't Work

Every long engagement contains things that failed. A case study with no failures in it has been edited into fiction.
