The Premise

Most search work fails for the same reason: someone bought a tool and nobody built the workflow around it. The dashboard gets checked on Mondays, the insights never reach anyone who can act on them, and eighteen months later the contract gets cancelled and blamed on the tool.

Every engagement here starts from the opposite end — what decision are you trying to make, what data would actually change it, and who on your team is going to own that loop when I'm gone.

That applies whether the surface is a Google results page or an AI answer. Search didn't get replaced, it got a second front. Your buyers now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews the same questions they used to type into a search box — and the brands getting cited in those answers are not always the brands ranking first.

  • checkYou've bought tooling — Profound, AirOps, Semrush, ClickUp — and it isn't earning its cost.

This doesn't work when

Common Questions

Coaching or done-for-you — which do I need?

If you have people and they're capable but stuck, coaching is faster and cheaper and the capability stays with you. If you have budget but no headcount, done-for-you gets results now. Most engagements start done-for-you and transition to coaching once the team can carry it.

Do you work with agencies or in-house teams?

Both, and the work differs. Agencies need repeatable process across many accounts. In-house teams need depth on one property and the internal credibility to get budget. I run those differently.

Is this SEO or GEO?

Both, because the split is already artificial. The same content decisions determine whether you rank in Google and whether you get cited in ChatGPT — but the measurement, the tooling and the failure modes are different, and most teams only learn half of it.

What if we already have tools we're not using properly?

That's the most common starting point. Teams buy Profound, AirOps, Semrush or Ahrefs, use ten percent of them, and conclude the tool doesn't work. The tool usually works. The workflow around it doesn't exist.
